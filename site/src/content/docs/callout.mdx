`kind` is `note` (the default), `warning` or `error`. Each has its own icon.

## Announcing

A callout has `role="note"`, so it isn't announced when it appears. A page that adds one puts it
in a live region already on the page.

<Demo label="A callout in a live region" stack>
  <div aria-live="polite">
    <Callout kind="error" title="Error">
      <p>Jackdaws love my big sphinx of quartz.</p>
    </Callout>
  </div>
</Demo>
