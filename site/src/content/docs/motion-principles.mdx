Motion explains: it answers input, shows work under way, or shows how a change happens. It is never
decoration.

## What never moves

- Readable text, layout and scroll position. Smooth scrolling is only for jumps within the page.
- The light, the outline width and the focus band.
- Nothing turns, so shadows stay on the light's side. A part that turns has no shadow: the spinner
  wedge, the dial's notch and the clock hands.

## Input responses

An input response starts in the same frame as the input, eases with plain `ease`, and never
repeats. Its length is a duration token:

- `--rkui-duration-fast`, 120ms: hover and press.
- `--rkui-duration-spring`, 180ms: toggles and snapping.
- `--rkui-duration-slide`, 320ms: travel and panels.

Only clickable things lift on hover and sink on press. Open, see DECISIONS.

## Ambient loops

An ambient loop starts by itself and runs while a state lasts, until the state ends or the reader
pauses it. One cycle is a loop token, and the loop holds still for a third or more of it:

- `--rkui-loop-short`, 0.9s: small busy marks.
- `--rkui-loop-medium`, 1.6s.
- `--rkui-loop-long`, 2.4s: fills and sloshes.
- `--rkui-loop-panel`, 4s: large areas and diagrams.

## Easing

- `ease` for input responses, `linear` for steady travel, `ease-out` going up and `ease-in` coming
  down for gravity, and `ease-in-out` with `alternate` for fills.
- `--rkui-ease-spring` recovers from a squash or a press.
- `--rkui-ease-snap` is a jump to a new place or state, with one visible overshoot of about 4%.

There are no other curves and no elastic easing.

## Toon phases

A move winds up, goes, overshoots and settles, with holds between. A sequence uses equal steps of
0.6 to 1s and ends on a held step.

- **Squash:** up to 1.3 × 0.7 on impact, 0.8–0.9 × 1.1–1.25 in flight, and 0.95 × 1.05 to settle.
  The area stays within about 10%, pinned to the contact point. The `rkui-squash` keyframes do this.
- **Overshoot:** one swing only, from the snap curve or one keyframe past the target, 10% at most.

## Stagger

Neighbours start `--rkui-stagger` (0.15s) apart, with `calc(var(--rkui-i) * var(--rkui-stagger))`,
in reading order or in the order a cause travels. An entrance spreads over 0.6s at most, and
staggered loops share one period.

## Shadows and performance

- Offsets that move with a shadow come from `--rkui-shadow-dx` and `--rkui-shadow-dy`, never literal
  numbers, and lengths that go with a shadow multiply by `--rkui-shadow-depth`.
- Animate `transform` and `opacity`, and never animate layout in a loop.
- At a join, move with inset offsets rather than `translate`: a `translate` transition can open a
  hairline seam.

## Reduced motion

Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`:

- the duration tokens become 0ms and the press and lift offsets 0px, so a transition timed with
  them happens at once and only the shadow changes;
- loops that show a state (spinners, loaders, pending panels) stay still and fade, with
  `rkui-spinner-fade` over 1.8s;
- illustrative loops stop. Open, see DECISIONS.

The loop tokens don't change, so an animation that loops has its own `prefers-reduced-motion`
rule, as each loop on this page does.

## Pausing

Self-moving content that runs longer than five seconds, other than a loader, has a pause control
(WCAG 2.2.2). The Motion component gives a group of animations one Pause button: while it is
pressed, every CSS animation inside the group pauses, including ones the page writes itself.
Transitions, animations started from a script (`element.animate()`), SVG `<animate>` and canvas
loops keep running.

## Your own animations

- Time transitions with the duration tokens, so reduced motion stops them.
- Time loops with the loop tokens, and give each one a `prefers-reduced-motion` rule.
- Colour a chart or canvas library's marks with the tokens, and turn off its own animations or time
  them with the tokens.
