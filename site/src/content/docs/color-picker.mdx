## Choosing the tiles

With no children the picker shows every hue. Give it ColorTile children for a smaller palette.

<Demo label="Colour picker with chosen tiles">
  <ColorPicker legend="Colour">
    <ColorTile value="teal" checked />
    <ColorTile value="coral" />
    <ColorTile value="grape" />
    <ColorTile value="yellow" />
  </ColorPicker>
</Demo>

## With a custom colour

The last tile opens the browser's colour picker.

## Flair A: paint brush

`flair="brush"` draws a faint paint brush on the chosen colour tile, and on a tile under the
pointer.

<Demo label="Colour picker, brush flair">
  <ColorPicker client:visible legend="Colour" value="teal" custom flair="brush" />
</Demo>

## Flair B: paint drops

`flair="drops"` shapes the tiles like drops of paint.

<Demo label="Colour picker, drops flair">
  <ColorPicker client:visible legend="Colour" value="teal" custom flair="drops" />
</Demo>

## Flair C: palette and stroke

`flair="stroke"` shows the legend with a palette icon, and a short stroke of the chosen colour
under its tile.

<Demo label="Colour picker, stroke flair">
  <ColorPicker client:visible legend="Colour" value="teal" custom flair="stroke" />
</Demo>
