Groups of links, each a child component. The page gives the sidebar its height; when its links
don't fit, it scrolls, with hints at its edges and a scrollbar on hover and focus. `variant`
picks the look.

The page's own colour, with dots at the edges; the current page is a tinted pill on a slab.
