Groups of links, each a child component. The page gives the sidebar its height; when its links
don't fit, it scrolls, with hints at its edges and a scrollbar on hover and focus. `variant`
picks the look; slab type, bold links, columns, link colours and icons work with any of them. `dots="pill"` puts the
hints in pills, as the card's are (see [Scroll](../scroll/)).

## With any look

Each look also takes these:

- `slab` on the Sidebar sets its header, headings and links in the slab serif.
- `selected-slab` on the Sidebar sets only the current page's link in the slab serif, and the
  other links in the UI font. Headings keep the look's own type.
- `bold` on the Sidebar sets its links bolder (weight 600).
- `columns` on the Sidebar lets each group's links flow into several columns when the sidebar is
  wide, as when it stacks above the page on a phone; a narrow sidebar keeps one column.
- `hue` on a SidebarLink gives the link its own colour, such as the section colour of the page it
  leads to. It fills the link's icon, and the link itself when it is the current page, in the
  look's own shape. Other links keep the page's text colour.
- An icon in a SidebarLink's `icon` slot sits before its text.
- The `header` slot stays above the links, for a site's name and tagline, and a SidebarGroup
  needs no heading.

## A site's own nav

The plain look on a sky panel (`--rkui-sidebar-panel`), with slab type, bold links, a header, one
group with no heading, and a colour and an icon for each link. With `columns` the links flow into
columns here, where the sidebar is as wide as the demo; in a narrow column they stay in one. In a flex column,
like this demo's, the sidebar needs an inline size, or the browser keeps the height of one column. The
[header band](../header-band/) page shows it in a site frame.
