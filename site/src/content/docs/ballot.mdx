Ballot works with no JavaScript. The small helper script only mends one thing in Safari: the focus band on a radio that an arrow key moves to. It does nothing in other browsers.

## Icons, several choices

Add `multiple` for checkboxes. Give an option a `hue` and `tint` to colour its checked box.

<Demo label="Ballot with icons" stack>
  <Ballot legend="Weather" multiple>
    <BallotOption value="sun" hue="yellow" tint checked>
      <Sun slot="icon" /> Sun
    </BallotOption>
    <BallotOption value="moon" hue="lilac" tint>
      <Moon slot="icon" /> Moon
    </BallotOption>
    <BallotOption value="leaf" hue="green" tint checked>
      <Leaf slot="icon" /> Leaf
    </BallotOption>
  </Ballot>
</Demo>

## Colours

A `hue` on an option with no icon or mark fills its tile with that colour.
