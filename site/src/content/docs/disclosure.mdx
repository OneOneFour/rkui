A `<details>` for an inline note. For storage that slides out of a panel, use a [Drawer](../drawer/).

<Demo label="A closed disclosure" stack>
  <Disclosure summary="Where this comes from">
    <p>Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs.</p>
  </Disclosure>
</Demo>

## Open

`open` starts it open.

<Demo label="An open disclosure" stack>
  <Disclosure summary="Where this comes from" open>
    <p>The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.</p>
  </Disclosure>
</Demo>

## One at a time

Disclosures that share a `name` open one at a time.

<Demo label="Disclosures that open one at a time" stack>
  <Disclosure summary="First" name="disclosure-group">
    <p>Sphinx of black quartz, judge my vow.</p>
  </Disclosure>
  <Disclosure summary="Second" name="disclosure-group">
    <p>How vexingly quick daft zebras jump.</p>
  </Disclosure>
</Demo>

## In a callout

Inside a callout it drops its rule, so several notes fold into one line.

<Demo label="Notes folded into one callout" stack>
  <Callout kind="warning" title="Two notes">
    <Disclosure summary="Show both">
      <ul>
        <li>Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs.</li>
        <li>The five boxing wizards jump quickly.</li>
      </ul>
    </Disclosure>
  </Callout>
</Demo>
