## Plain or pill dots

`dots="pill"` puts the dots in a raised pill.

<Demo vue={looksCode} label="Plain and pill dots" stack>

</Demo>

CodeBlock, Table, Folder, Pagination, Sidebar and Equation take the same `dots`.
