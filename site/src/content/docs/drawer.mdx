## On other sides

A drawer pulls out from the edge `placement` names. On top it comes first in its parent. On the left and right it hangs beyond the parent's side, as tall as the parent, so the parent's content stays put: leave room beside the parent for its handle and its tray. The tray opens as wide as its contents, up to `--rkui-drawer-width` (`min(12rem, 35vw)` when left out). A card holding one doesn't clip it; another parent needs `position: relative`, and mustn't clip it either.

## Remote

A remote drawer is opened by a button elsewhere on the page, with no JavaScript: the button's `popovertarget` names the drawer's `id`, and the browser tells screen readers whether it is open. The drawer's own front is no button: it shows only an arrow, and a blind's cord ends in an empty ring. A remote drawer always starts shut; a button inside it can shut it with `popovertargetaction="hide"`. With the helper script, Escape inside it shuts it too, and when it shuts with the focus inside, the focus goes back to the button that opens it.

## Over the page

With `overlay` a drawer on the top or bottom hangs over what lies beyond its parent's edge instead of pushing it away, with its own fill, outline and shadow, as one on the left or right always does. It is placed against the edge of its containing block, so give the parent `position: relative`, and don't let the parent clip it (a card does, so this suits a bar or a panel). It is as wide as the parent; set its `inset` to make it narrower or move it along the edge. The docs site's settings drawer is one, opened by the Settings button in the top bar.
