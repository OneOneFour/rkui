Hover or focus the button to show the tooltip. It needs no JavaScript.

<Demo label="Tooltip">
  <div style="padding-block-start: var(--rkui-space-7)">
    <Tooltip id="tooltip-save" text="Save the file">
      <Button aria-describedby="tooltip-save">Save</Button>
    </Tooltip>
  </div>
</Demo>

## Flat

`variant="flat"` swaps the page's colours, with no outline or shadow.

<Demo label="Flat tooltip">
  <div style="padding-block-start: var(--rkui-space-7)">
    <Tooltip id="tooltip-flat" text="Save the file" variant="flat">
      <Button aria-describedby="tooltip-flat">Save</Button>
    </Tooltip>
  </div>
</Demo>
