## Static edges

`variant` sets a static card's edge: `outline` (the default), `raised`, `shaded` or `inset`.

## Heading level

`headingLevel` (2, 3 or 4) sets the title's heading level; 3 when left out.

<Demo label="Cards with heading levels">
  <div style="display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap; gap: var(--rkui-space-6)">

      <p>An h2 title.</p>
    </Card>
    <Card title="Level 4" headingLevel={4} style="inline-size: 12rem">
      <p>An h4 title.</p>
    </Card>
  </div>
</Demo>
