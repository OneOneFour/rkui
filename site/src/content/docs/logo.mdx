`Logo` renders the rkui logo as a home link, raised on the hard shadow. It lifts on hover and sinks
when pressed.

<Demo vue={liftCode} label="Logo">
  <Lift />
</Demo>

## Motifs

With `variant="motifs"`, hover and keyboard focus play each tile's motif: the folded corner lifts,
the wave rolls, the stripes march and the dot of the i hops.

<Demo vue={motifsCode} label="Logo with motifs">
  <Motifs />
</Demo>

## Without a link

Leave out `href` and the logo is a plain `<span>`: it doesn't pop, and it plays its motifs all the
time (not under reduced motion).

</Demo>
