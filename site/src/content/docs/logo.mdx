`Logo` renders the rkui logo as a home link, raised on the hard shadow. It lifts on hover and sinks
when pressed.

<Demo vue={liftCode} label="Logo">
  <Lift />
</Demo>

## Motifs

With `variant="motifs"`, hover and keyboard focus play each tile's motif: the folded corner lifts,
the wave rolls, the stripes march and the dot of the i hops.

<Demo vue={motifsCode} label="Logo with motifs">
  <Motifs />
</Demo>

## Without a link

Leave out `href` and the logo is a plain `<span>` named "rkui". It doesn't pop, since it can't be
clicked. It plays every motif once as the page loads, then again while the pointer is over it.
Under reduced motion it stays still.

<Demo vue={staticCode} label="Logo without a link">
  <Static />
</Demo>
