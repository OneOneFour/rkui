`Quote` renders a `<blockquote>` on a slate panel, in Roboto Slab. It is as wide as its column,
with no indent, so its edges line up with the text around it, and it keeps the same space above
and below. With `by`, the attribution goes under the quote in a `<figcaption>`.

<Demo label="Quote with an attribution" stack>
  <Quote by="Ada Lovelace, 1843">
    <p>
      The Analytical Engine weaves algebraical patterns just as the Jacquard-loom weaves flowers and
      leaves.
    </p>
  </Quote>
</Demo>

## Short quotes

A quote whose text fits on one line is set two steps larger (`--rkui-step-2`), and one of two
lines a step larger (`--rkui-step-1`), by the helper script `quote-enhance.js`. It measures each quote at the normal size, in the column it sits in, so
the same quote can be larger in a wide column and normal on a phone, where it wraps to three lines
or more. Its padding and the space around it stay the same. Without the script, every quote keeps
the normal size.

<Demo label="A short quote and a long one" stack>
  <Quote by="Short">
    <p>How vexingly quick daft zebras jump.</p>
  </Quote>
  <Quote by="Long">
    <p>
      Pack my box with five dozen liquor jugs. The five boxing wizards jump quickly. Sphinx of black
      quartz, judge my vow. Jackdaws love my big sphinx of quartz. The quick onyx goblin jumps over
      the lazy dwarf. Five quacking zephyrs jolt my wax bed.
    </p>
  </Quote>
</Demo>

`short` sets a quote two steps larger whatever its length, for a pull quote that should stay large
on a phone too.

<Demo label="A short quote that stays large" stack>
  <Quote short by="Short">
    <p>Sphinx of black quartz, judge my vow, and the five boxing wizards jump quickly.</p>
  </Quote>
</Demo>

## Slant

A quote is upright by default. Roboto Slab has no italic, so `slant="italic"` has the browser slant
its upright letters. Emphasis inside an italic quote turns upright.

<Demo label="Upright and italic quotes" stack>
  <Quote by="Upright">
    <p>
      Sphinx of black quartz, <em>judge</em> my vow.
    </p>
  </Quote>
  <Quote slant="italic" by="Italic">
    <p>
      Sphinx of black quartz, <em>judge</em> my vow.
    </p>
  </Quote>
</Demo>

## Markdown quotes

On this site a Markdown `>` quote renders as a `Quote`:

> How vexingly quick daft zebras jump.

A site does the same by mapping `blockquote` to `Quote` where it renders its Markdown and MDX:

```astro
---
import Quote from '@oneonefour/rkui/components/quote/Quote.vue'
import TryIt from '../../examples/quote/TryIt.vue'
import tryItCode from '../../examples/quote/TryIt.vue?raw'
const { Content } = await render(entry)
---

<Content components={{ blockquote: Quote }} />
```

or in one MDX file:

```mdx
import Quote from '@oneonefour/rkui/components/quote/Quote.vue'
import TryIt from '../../examples/quote/TryIt.vue'
import tryItCode from '../../examples/quote/TryIt.vue?raw'
export const components = { blockquote: Quote }
```

A Markdown quote has no `by`; write `<Quote by="…">` for an attribution.
