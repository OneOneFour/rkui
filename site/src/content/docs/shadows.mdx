## Light

`--rkui-light-angle` says where the light comes from, clockwise from the top: `315deg`, the top
left, by default. Shadows fall away from it, on `<html>` or inside any element that sets it
([Settings](../settings/)). `LightDirection` turns it for the whole page and stores it: drag the
sun round the compass, or focus it and use the arrow keys. The block in the middle casts the
page's shadow.

<Demo label="Turning the light">
  <LightDirection client:visible />
  <Button>A button</Button>
</Demo>

<Demo label="A section lit from the top right">
  <div style="--rkui-light-angle: 45deg">
    <Button>Lit from the top right</Button>
  </div>
</Demo>

## Dark mode

`data-shadow` on any container sets how shadows look in dark mode: `outline` (the default),
`block` or `color`. Light mode is the same in all three.

<Demo vue={darkShadowsCode} label="The three dark-mode shadows" stack widget>
  <DarkShadows />
</Demo>

`data-highlight` sets the outline on things with no fill in dark mode: `cream` (the default) or
`dark`.

<Demo vue={highlightCode} label="The two dark-mode highlights" stack widget>
  <Highlight />
</Demo>

## Your own element

`box-shadow: var(--rkui-shadow)` gives an element the hard shadow. With `data-shadow="color"`, it
casts its own colour once it sets `--rkui-shade-of` to its fill and `--rkui-extrusion:
var(--rkui-shade)`.
