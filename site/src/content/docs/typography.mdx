Headings are Roboto Slab at weight 800, body and interface text is Xmind, and code is JetBrains
Mono. Use them through `--rkui-font-display`, `--rkui-font-body` and `--rkui-font-mono`.

  <p style="margin: 0; font-family: var(--rkui-font-body)">

  </p>
</Demo>

## Scale

Each step is the one below it times 1.3, from `--rkui-step-0` at 1rem. Headings `h1` to `h4` use
steps 4 to 1; small text is `--rkui-step--1`, fixed at 0.8rem.
