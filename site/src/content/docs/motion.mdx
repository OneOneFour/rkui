## Input responses

- `--rkui-duration-fast`: 120ms
- `--rkui-duration-spring`: 180ms
- `--rkui-duration-slide`: 320ms

<Demo vue={responseCode} label="A button's input response, and the same under reduced motion">
  <Response />
</Demo>

## Ambient loops

- `--rkui-loop-quick`: 0.6s
- `--rkui-loop-short`: 0.9s
- `--rkui-loop-steady`: 1.2s
- `--rkui-loop-medium`: 1.8s
- `--rkui-loop-long`: 2.4s
- `--rkui-loop-panel`: 4s

## Pausing: the Motion component

`<Motion>` puts a Pause button above its contents that pauses their CSS animations. It needs no
JavaScript.

<Demo label="A Motion group with its Pause button">
  <Motion>
    <div style="display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap; align-items: center; gap: var(--rkui-space-6); margin-block-start: var(--rkui-space-4)">
      <Spinner />
      <Dots />
      <Flask />
      <Loader />
    </div>
  </Motion>
</Demo>
