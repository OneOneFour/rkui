Things move to explain: to answer the reader's input, to show work under way, or to show how a
change happens. Nothing moves for decoration. This page covers the timing tokens, the easing
curves, reduced motion, and the Motion component, which gives a group of animations a Pause
button.

## What never moves

- Readable text, the layout and the scroll position. Smooth scrolling is only for jumps within the
  page.
- The light, the outline width and the focus band.
- Nothing turns while it casts a shadow, so shadows always fall away from the light. Parts that
  turn have no shadow: the spinner's wedge, the dial's notch and the clock's hands.

## Input responses

An input response starts in the same frame as the input, eases with plain `ease`, and plays once.
Its length is a duration token:

- `--rkui-duration-fast`, 120ms: hover and press.
- `--rkui-duration-spring`, 180ms: toggles and snapping.
- `--rkui-duration-slide`, 320ms: travel and panels.

Clickable things lift on hover and sink on press; things that can't be clicked stay still. Two
options lift on hover without a click: a Table's `lift` and a StickyNote's `animated`.

## Ambient loops

An ambient loop starts by itself and runs while a state lasts, until the state ends or the reader
pauses it. One cycle is a loop token, and the loop holds still for at least a third of it:

- `--rkui-loop-quick`, 0.6s: a hop, or a stripe's march.
- `--rkui-loop-short`, 0.9s: small busy marks.
- `--rkui-loop-steady`, 1.2s: a turn, or a rolling wave.
- `--rkui-loop-medium`, 1.8s: a circuit, and the reduced-motion fade.
- `--rkui-loop-long`, 2.4s: fills and sloshes.
- `--rkui-loop-panel`, 4s: large areas and diagrams.

## Easing

- `ease` for input responses, `linear` for steady travel, `ease-out` going up and `ease-in` coming
  down under gravity, and `ease-in-out` with `alternate` for fills.
- `--rkui-ease-spring` recovers from a squash or a press.
- `--rkui-ease-snap` jumps to a new place or state, with one visible overshoot of about 4%.

There are no other curves and no elastic easing.

## Toon phases

A move winds up, goes, overshoots and settles, with holds between. A sequence takes equal steps of
0.6 to 1s and ends on a held step.

- **Squash:** up to 1.3 × 0.7 on impact, 0.8–0.9 × 1.1–1.25 in flight, and 0.95 × 1.05 to settle.
  The area stays within about 10%, pinned to the point of contact. The library's `rkui-squash`
  keyframes do this.
- **Overshoot:** one swing only, 10% at most, from the snap curve or one keyframe past the target.

## Stagger

Neighbours start `--rkui-stagger` (0.15s) apart, with `calc(var(--rkui-i) * var(--rkui-stagger))`,
in reading order or in the order a cause travels. An entrance spreads over 0.6s at most, and
staggered loops share one period.

## Reduced motion

When the reader asks for reduced motion (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`):

- the duration tokens become 0ms and the press and lift offsets 0px, so a transition timed with
  them happens at once and only the shadow changes;
- loops that show a state (spinners, loaders, pending panels) stay in place and fade in and out,
  with `rkui-spinner-fade` over `--rkui-loop-medium`;
- loops that only illustrate something stop.

The loop tokens stay the same, so each looping animation has its own `prefers-reduced-motion`
rule, as each loop on this page does.

## Pausing: the Motion component

Anything that moves by itself for more than five seconds, other than a loader, needs a way to
pause it (WCAG 2.2.2). Wrap it in `<Motion>`, which puts a Pause button above it. While Pause is
pressed, every CSS animation inside the group pauses, including ones the page writes itself;
animations elsewhere on the page keep running. A group inside another pauses with it, and its own
Pause button pauses only itself.

Motion works without JavaScript, so in an Astro page it needs no `client:` directive. It pauses CSS
animations only: transitions, animations started from a script (`element.animate()`), SVG
`<animate>` and canvas drawing keep running.

## Your own animations

- Time transitions with the duration tokens, so that reduced motion stops them.
- Time loops with the loop tokens, and give each one a `prefers-reduced-motion` rule.
- Move a shadow's offsets with `--rkui-shadow-dx` and `--rkui-shadow-dy`, never fixed numbers, and
  multiply lengths that go with a shadow by `--rkui-shadow-depth`.
- Animate `transform` and `opacity`, and never animate the layout in a loop.
- Where two pieces meet, move them with inset offsets rather than `translate`: a `translate`
  transition can open a hairline seam between them.
- Colour a chart or canvas library's marks with the tokens, and turn off its own animations or time
  them with the tokens.
